Company Profile

About Us

Superior Construction &
Development Corporation

Established in May 2025, Superior Construction and Development Corporation (SCDC) is a Philippine-based construction support and aggregate supply company committed to delivering premium-quality materials and reliable operational services to the country's infrastructure industry.

Today, SCDC supplies high-quality aggregates for major infrastructure, commercial, industrial, and residential developments throughout Luzon and Metro Manila.

SCDC is a subsidiary of Superior Motor Corporation (SMC), operating successfully since 2003.

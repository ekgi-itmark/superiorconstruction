What We Operate

Crushing & Screening

Our crusher plant processes raw quarried rock through multiple stages — jaw crushing, cone crushing, and vibrating screens — to produce graded aggregates across a range of sizes. Each batch is tested against DPWH standards before leaving.

  • Jaw crusher — primary reduction stage
  • Cone crusher — secondary & tertiary refinement
  • Multi-deck vibrating screen classification
  • Dedicated sand-making unit for fine aggregates

Capacity & Consistency

The plant operates continuously during project peak demand with output scaled to client volume requirements. All crushed products maintain consistent gradation — reducing rejection rates and rework on your site.

  • Consistent sieve gradation batch to batch
  • Scalable output — small to bulk project volumes
  • Rapid turnaround for urgent project demands
  • On-site stockpiling for ready order fulfilment

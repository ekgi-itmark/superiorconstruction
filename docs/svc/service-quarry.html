What We Operate

Extraction & Processing

We run a fully licensed quarrying operation across 22 hectares of productive land in Batangas. Our site produces a range of coarse and fine aggregates — from raw boulders to graded gravel — through a controlled extraction workflow.

  • Controlled blasting & excavation
  • Primary rock extraction — granite & limestone
  • On-site material classification and grading
  • Environmental compliance monitoring

Why Source Direct

Sourcing from your own licensed quarry means consistent quality, shorter lead times, and better pricing — no middlemen, no supply uncertainty. Every aggregate comes directly from ground we control and maintain.

  • Direct pricing — no markup layers
  • Consistent grading batch to batch
  • Immediate stock visibility for project planning
  • Scalable output for large infrastructure volumes

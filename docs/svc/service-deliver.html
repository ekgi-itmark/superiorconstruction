How We Deliver

Direct Quarry-to-Site

All deliveries are fulfilled by dump trucks dispatched directly from our Batangas quarry. We coordinate scheduling around your project timeline — whether a single truckload or sustained supply across months of construction.

  • Direct source delivery — no intermediaries
  • Flexible scheduling — daily, weekly, or on-call
  • Delivery receipts and documentation provided
  • Luzon-wide coverage including Metro Manila

Coverage Area

Based in Sto. Tomas, Batangas — with direct access to STAR Tollway, SLEX, and EDSA — we efficiently reach project sites throughout Luzon and Metro Manila.

  • Batangas, Cavite, Laguna — primary zone
  • Metro Manila via SLEX / EDSA corridor
  • Provincial Luzon — coordinated scheduling
  • Site access assessed per order
